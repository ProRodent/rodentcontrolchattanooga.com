Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: May 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Rodent Control Chattanooga (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit rodentcontrolchattanooga.com (the “Site”). We operate a call-only pest-control lead-generation website. The Site has no contact forms, no email capture fields, and no user account system.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide directly. The only way to contact us through this Site is by telephone. When you call (844) 635-0403, your call may be connected through a call-tracking system (Ringba) that records the fact of the call, its duration, and the originating phone number for business and analytics purposes. We do not record call audio without disclosure on the call itself.

Automatically collected information. Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare Pages) automatically collects standard server-log information including your IP address, browser type, referring URL, and pages visited. This data is used for security, performance monitoring, and aggregate traffic analysis. We do not operate third-party advertising pixels or behavior-tracking scripts.

Cookies. This Site does not set first-party tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set a security cookie (__cf_bm) for bot-management purposes. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any similar third-party tracking services.

2. How we use information

  • To route and answer your phone call and schedule a service inspection
  • To monitor and maintain the technical performance of the Site
  • To detect and prevent abusive or malicious traffic via Cloudflare’s security layer
  • To analyze aggregate (non-personally-identifiable) call and traffic volume for business planning

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Call tracking (Ringba)

Phone numbers displayed on this Site may be tracked through Ringba, a call analytics platform. Ringba records call metadata (originating number, call duration, time and date) to help us understand which pages and campaigns generate service inquiries. Ringba’s privacy practices are governed by Ringba’s Privacy Policy. If you prefer not to have your call tracked, you may call our direct business line. Contact us by email to request it.

4. Cloudflare

This Site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and served through Cloudflare’s content delivery network. Cloudflare processes your IP address and HTTP request data for performance and security purposes. Their practices are governed by Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

5. Data retention

Server log data is retained by Cloudflare per their standard retention periods. Call metadata collected through Ringba is retained for up to 24 months for business analytics purposes and then deleted or anonymized. We do not maintain a customer database on this website.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Because we collect minimal data (server logs and call metadata only), the practical scope of these rights is limited. To make a request, email us at contact@rodentcontrolchattanooga.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Residents of California may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.

7. Children’s privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Links to third-party sites

This Site may contain links to third-party websites (for example, Google Maps embedded on the Contact page). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated&rdquo. Date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after any change is acceptance of the updated policy.
