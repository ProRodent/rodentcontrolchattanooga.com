About the company

A locally-owned rodent control company built for Chattanooga

Rodent Control Chattanooga is a locally-owned rat and mouse removal company founded in 2023 and operating from a base on McCallie Avenue in the heart of Hamilton County. We focus on one thing, rodent removal and exclusion, for one region: Chattanooga, its 28 neighborhoods, and the 20 truly adjacent towns within roughly 45 minutes of downtown. We do not chase work outside that radius, and we do not subcontract jobs to other companies.

The company started because the existing options in Chattanooga were split between two extremes: massive national franchises with call centers that route Hamilton County jobs through out-of-state schedulers, and one-truck operators who handle the trap-and-leave job but don’t come back to seal the entry points. Neither fit how the rodent pressure actually works here.

Chattanooga’s rodent problems are shaped by geography. The Tennessee River curves through the city between the ridges of Lookout Mountain, Signal Mountain, and Missionary Ridge. Humid subtropical weather, mature tree canopy across the historic neighborhoods, and storm-season runoff from the ridges all push rodents in patterns that change by season and by street. A St. Elmo roof rat problem in October needs different work than a downtown restaurant Norway rat problem in July. A Hixson suburban mouse infestation in December is not the same job as a Brainerd crawl-space issue in March.

The company was built around that reality: every job starts with a free property inspection so we can see what species, what entry points, what season, and what structure we’re actually dealing with, before we recommend treatment or quote a number.

Regulatory note

Tennessee pest control regulations &. How we handle them

Tennessee structural pest control is regulated by the state, and chemical treatments, including rodenticides used inside structures, require compliance with state rules under the Department of Agriculture’s pesticide program. We follow those rules on every job that involves restricted-use products.

Where the work is purely mechanical (snap-traps, exclusion sealing, dead rodent removal, decontamination) the regulatory bar is different and we operate within it. For rodenticide-bait work, we use tamper-resistant exterior stations rated for residential and commercial use, applied in line with current TN structural pest control guidance.

If you have specific questions about what products will be used on your property, ask during the free inspection, every recommendation comes with the rationale, not just the product name.

What we don’t do

The boundaries of the company, stated up front

To save everyone time, here is what falls outside our scope. We will refer you to a better-fit operator for any of these:

  • General pest control for ants, roaches, termites, mosquitoes, or wildlife (raccoons, opossums, bats, snakes). We handle rats, mice, and the structural fallout of rodent infestations, not the full general-pest catalog.
  • Bird control (pigeons, sparrows, starlings). Different equipment, different exclusion approach.
  • Out-of-region work. We do not service Nashville, Knoxville, Birmingham, or Atlanta. If you’re outside the 45-minute radius from Chattanooga, we’ll point you toward a closer operator.
  • Tenant-side work without owner authorization. For rentals, we need authorization from the property owner or property manager before scheduling a treatment plan.
